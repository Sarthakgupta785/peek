What is Peek?

Peek is a free, self-hosted app that lets a person check on their own computer from their phone: whether it is on, whether someone is using it, which apps are open, and alerts when something happens while they are away.

Each copy of Peek runs on one person's computer and is used only by that person. This address is a private Peek dashboard; signing in is limited to its owner.

How sign-in works

Before the dashboard opens, you sign in with Google. Peek only asks Google for your email address and basic profile, and uses them for one thing: checking that you are the owner allowed to open this dashboard. After that, the dashboard asks for its own password and a code from an authenticator app.
