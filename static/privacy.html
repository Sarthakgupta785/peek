Privacy policy

Last updated 21 November 2026

Who runs this

This Peek dashboard is run by one individual, on their own computer, for their own personal use. It is not a commercial service and has no other users.

What Google sign-in shares

When you sign in with Google, Peek receives your email address, name and profile picture (the email and profile scopes). Peek uses only the email address, to check that it is on the owner's allow-list. Anyone else is refused. Peek does not ask for access to your Gmail, Drive, contacts or any other Google data.

What Peek stores

  • Information about the owner's own computer: CPU, memory, disk and network use, which watched apps are open, and events such as when it started or woke up.
  • Security records: sign-in attempts with the time, IP address and device type, so the owner can spot someone trying to get in.

All of it stays on the owner's computer, in a local file. Secrets are encrypted on disk, and screenshots are encrypted end to end so only the owner's phone can open them.

Sharing

Peek does not sell, rent or share any data, shows no ads and has no analytics or trackers. Traffic passes through ngrok (to reach the computer) and Google (to sign in); their own privacy policies apply to that part.

Keeping and deleting data

Computer stats are kept for 30 days and events for 180 days, then deleted automatically. The owner can delete everything at any time by removing Peek's data folder. To remove Peek's access to your Google account, visit myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact

Questions about this dashboard go to its owner, using the support email shown on Google's sign-in screen for Peek.
